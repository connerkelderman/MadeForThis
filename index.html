The Journey

What to expect

Working together is simple and personal. Here's how our relationship unfolds from the very first conversation.

01

Free Discovery Call

A relaxed 20–30 minute conversation to get to know each other, talk about your birth vision, and see if we're a great fit. No pressure, no commitment — just a warm chat.

02

Prenatal Visits

We'll meet one-on-one to dive into your birth preferences, practice comfort techniques, and create a plan that reflects exactly what you want. Partners are always welcome.

03

On-Call & Labor Support

Starting at 38 weeks, I'm on call around the clock. When labor begins, I'll join you at your chosen location and stay by your side with continuous support — from early labor through delivery and golden hour.

04

Postpartum Follow-Up

After baby arrives, I'll visit you at home to check in on recovery, help with early feeding, answer questions, and make sure you feel confident and supported in your new rhythm.
